# Contributing

Contributions are welcome if they improve the skill while preserving its independent authorship and user-first design stance.

## Contribution rules

1. Write in your own words.
2. Use your own examples.
3. Do not copy book text, diagrams, screenshots, exercises, tables, or chapter structures.
4. Do not submit converted PDFs, EPUBs, scans, or source-book Markdown.
5. Do not add author or book branding to the project identity.
6. Prefer general methods over source-specific terminology when a neutral description works.
7. Keep engagement recommendations transparent, voluntary, and easy to leave.
8. Include accessibility, error recovery, and validation where relevant.

## Before submitting

Check that your change:

- improves a real product-design task,
- is concrete and testable,
- does not create unnecessary cognitive burden,
- does not introduce dark patterns,
- is independently worded,
- does not require a proprietary runtime unless clearly optional.

## Rights

By contributing, you represent that you have the right to submit your contribution under the repository's MIT License and that it does not knowingly include material you are not permitted to redistribute.
